About Me

I'm Dan Moses, known online as Daentz Decracker, a Computer Science student at Maasai Mara University, Kenya. I build for the web because I like watching an idea turn into something people can click around in.

My strength is the frontend: clean layouts, smooth interactions, interfaces that feel alive. Now I'm pushing into the backend with Node.js, Express and MongoDB to build complete systems.

I'm drawn to anime-inspired design. The bold colors, energy and glitch you see across this site come from that.
